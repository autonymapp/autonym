AI roleplay & story-writing, without the technical mess.

Autonym is a structured, compendium-first desktop app for solo roleplayers and authors. Build a Cast from labeled fields instead of prompt engineering. Pace scenes with Beat Sheets and Mood presets, and let dynamic Lorebooks keep your world consistent — all running on your own OpenRouter key.

Autonym is in active beta — the core experience is stable and used daily by its own developer, but you may still hit rough edges. Bug reports and feedback are very welcome.

What's inside

Everything a long-form roleplay needs, none of the busywork.

Eight systems that work together — from a first character sheet to a finished, exported story.

Beat Sheets & Mood presets

A drag-and-drop scene roadmap paired with one-click steering — Slow Burn, High Action & Grit, Cinematic & Atmospheric, or Slice of Life / Banter — that resists the model's urge to resolve tension too fast.

Dynamic Lorebooks

Cue-word-triggered world-info injection, with an AI-assisted "Fill from Wiki/Text" importer that recognizes Fandom wiki markup for instant, one-paste entries.

New

Universes & linked variants

Group your Cast by world or verse, then link a canon or AU take on a character to a base version that shares personality automatically — no duplicate sheets.

New

Skits

Unattended short-story generation — pick a premise and a length, and the AI writes a complete short scene end to end. Ideal for ship fic without the typing.

Curated, RP-tuned models

Built-in presets for current-gen Claude, Magnum, and Euryale. Filtered against OpenRouter's live catalog so dead or unsuitable models never waste your time, with transparent per-message cost estimates as you type.

Prompt caching

Automatic prompt caching on supported Claude models keeps lore-heavy, long-running scenes fast and cheap.

Direct export

Export a single Act or a whole combined Storyline straight to EPUB, PDF, or Markdown — great for reading on an e-reader or keeping a permanent archive.

Fully local, privacy-first

Your data stays on your machine. Your OpenRouter key is encrypted at rest with OS-level encryption and never leaves the main process in plaintext.

How it's built

Your key, your machine, your story.

Encrypted key storage

Your OpenRouter API key is encrypted at rest via OS-level credential encryption and is only ever used from Electron's main process.

No account, no telemetry

There's no Autonym account and nothing phones home. You talk to OpenRouter directly, on the model of your choice, at the price it quotes.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is Autonym really free and open-source?

Yes. Autonym is 100% free and MIT-licensed. There are no paywalled features, subscription tiers, or hidden charges built into the app.

Is Autonym in beta? What does that mean for me?

Yes — Autonym is in active beta. The core experience is stable and used daily by its own developer, but you may still run into rough edges as it's used across different setups, models, and workflows. Bug reports and feature requests via GitHub Issues genuinely shape what gets fixed next.

How does billing work if there's no subscription?

You bring your own OpenRouter API key. Instead of paying Autonym a monthly fee, you pay OpenRouter directly, only for the exact text tokens you use — typically just a few cents per massive roleplay response.

Where is my data and API key stored?

Everything stays entirely on your machine. Your OpenRouter API key is encrypted at rest using OS-level secure credential storage, and your chat logs and lorebooks never leave your local file system.

Is my roleplay content private?

Yes. Nothing you write is seen by Autonym's developer or sent anywhere except the OpenRouter endpoint and model you've chosen, to generate a reply — governed by OpenRouter's and that model provider's own privacy terms, not Autonym's.

What models work best with Autonym?

Autonym features built-in presets for current-gen frontier models and community favorites like Claude, Magnum, and Euryale over OpenRouter. The app filters the catalog so dead or incompatible models don't clutter your workflow — or you can enter any OpenRouter model ID manually.

Does it support prompt caching?

Yes. For supported models like Claude, automatic prompt caching keeps lore-heavy, long-running roleplay sessions fast and cheap by avoiding redundant token processing.

What operating systems are supported?

Autonym is built as a native desktop application, currently packaged for Windows only while in beta. macOS and Linux builds are being considered based on demand — let us know if you'd use one.

How can I support the project?

Autonym is free with no ads or paywalls, so any support is entirely optional and doesn't unlock anything extra. If you'd like to chip in, you can do so on Ko-fi — otherwise, starring the repo or just using it and reporting bugs helps just as much.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub — that's the best and fastest way to reach the developer directly, especially during beta. For softer, less formal thoughts, use the feedback form below instead.

What's the difference between a GitHub Issue and the feedback form?

A GitHub Issue is for something concrete and trackable — a bug with steps to reproduce, or a specific feature request — and it's public. The feedback form is for anything looser: general impressions, "I wish X worked differently," or just letting us know what's working. No GitHub account needed, and it's not public.
