# Screenshots checklist

I can't capture these myself (no way to drive the actual app window from here) — here's exactly
what to grab and how to name it, matching the app's real screens and terminology. Aim for 1080p+
PNG, at the app's default window size, Midnight Indigo theme.

- `01_beat_sheet_scenario_maker.png` — Scenario Maker, with a Beat Sheet mid-drag or freshly
  reordered, showing the pacing roadmap.
- `02_lorebooks_cue_words.png` — Lorebooks page, an entry open showing its Cue Words and (if
  handy) the Canon Setting toggle.
- `03_act_narrative_view.png` — An Act in Narrative mode, mid-scene, showing the dialogue/action/
  thought formatting rendered (not just the composer).
- `04_model_styles_pricing.png` — The Model tab in an Act's settings panel, showing an RP-tuned
  Style selected and the live per-message cost estimate.

Good second-tier shots if you want more than four: the Universes page with linked-variant chips,
a Skit mid-generation, and the Mood & Pacing / Content Intensity controls.
