# Screenshots checklist

I can't capture these myself (no way to drive the actual app window from here) — here's exactly
what to grab and how to name it, matching the app's real screens and terminology. Aim for 1080p+
PNG, at the app's default window size, Midnight Indigo theme.

- `01_waypoints_journeys.png` — Journeys, with a Waypoint mid-drag or freshly reordered, showing
  the pacing roadmap.
- `02_records_cue_words.png` — Records page, an entry open showing its Cue Words and (if
  handy) the Canon Setting toggle.
- `03_transmission_narrative_view.png` — A Transmission in Narrative mode, mid-scene, showing the
  dialogue/action/thought formatting rendered (not just the composer).
- `04_model_styles_pricing.png` — The Model tab in a Transmission's settings panel, showing an
  RP-tuned Style selected and the live per-message cost estimate.

Good second-tier shots if you want more than four: the Universes page with linked-variant chips,
a Beacon mid-generation, and the Mood & Pacing / Content Intensity controls.
