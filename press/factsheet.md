# Autonym — Factsheet

**App Name:** Autonym
**Tagline:** AI roleplay and story-writing, without the technical mess.
**Platform:** Windows (Electron / TypeScript)
**Backend:** OpenRouter integration (bring your own key, pay-per-message)
**License:** MIT — free and open-source
**Availability:** Public preview

## Core Pitch

Autonym is a structured, compendium-first desktop writing environment for solo roleplayers and
authors. Instead of forcing writers to master raw prompt engineering or navigate a cluttered chat
wrapper, Autonym paces scenes through interactive Beat Sheets and a one-click Mood/Pacing selector,
injects lore automatically through cue-word-tagged Lorebooks, and shows transparent per-message
pricing with prompt caching on supported models.

## Key Features

- **Beat Sheets & Mood/Pacing presets** — a pacing-aware AI that resists the urge to resolve
  tension too fast; one-click steering (Slow Burn, High Action & Grit, Cinematic & Atmospheric,
  Slice of Life/Banter) on top of a drag-and-drop Beat Sheet roadmap.
- **Dynamic Lorebooks** — cue-word-triggered world-info injection, with AI-assisted "Fill from
  Wiki/Text" that recognizes Fandom's wiki markup for one-paste imports.
- **Universes & linked character variants** — group Cast by world/verse, and link a canon or AU
  take on a character to a base version that shares personality automatically.
- **Skits** — unattended short-story generation: pick a premise and length, the AI writes a
  complete short scene end to end, exportable straight to EPUB.
- **Built-in RP-tuned model presets** — current-generation Claude, Magnum, and Euryale presets,
  each with transparent live per-message cost estimates.
- **Prompt caching** on supported Claude models, for fast, low-cost lore-heavy scenes.
- **Direct export** to EPUB, PDF, and Markdown — for a single Act or a whole combined Storyline.
- **Fully local, privacy-first** — your data stays on your machine; your OpenRouter key is
  encrypted at rest with OS-level encryption and never leaves it.
